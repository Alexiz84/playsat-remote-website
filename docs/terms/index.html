1. Features and compatibility

PlaySat Remote lets you discover, pair with and control compatible televisions and streaming devices. Depending on the device and platform, it provides navigation, power commands, volume and channel controls, media controls, text entry, app shortcuts, touchpad or pointer control, local-media casting and access to screen-mirroring options.

Features depend on the phone, TV, operating system, firmware, receiver software, media format, network and permissions. A supported TV brand does not mean every model or feature is supported. Power-on normally requires a supported wake method and network standby settings; the App cannot turn on a TV disconnected from power. App lists may depend on information exposed by the TV, and some apps cannot be launched remotely.

Local control and casting generally require your phone and TV to be on the same local network. Screen mirroring may use a phone's built-in wireless-display feature or a separate service such as Smart View or Google Home. Availability and any additional setup are shown in the relevant flow. The App cannot bypass content protection or guarantee that protected video or audio can be mirrored. Buying Pro unlocks supported App features; it does not add missing hardware or TV capabilities.

PlaySat Remote does not supply TV channels, films, IPTV subscriptions or subscriptions to streaming services. Content services may require their own accounts or payments.
